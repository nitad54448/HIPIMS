Workflow
  1. Sweep around the resonance (≈6 MHz) and choose the Lorentz or circle fit.
  2. PLL lock: advisor gains (sign from the slope polarity), lock, record frequency.
  3. Q control (optional): gain calibration by sweeps of the effective resonance, target Q (PLL off while calibrating).
  4. HiPIMS: step the measurement window against the plasma pulse.
  5. Analysis: Sauerbrey / Z-match thickness and rate, ion-flux fraction, report.

Gain calibration

Method of the Zurich Instruments blog: the X and Y of the response are fed back at 0° and 90° (two PIDs, one output) with P = −gain. Gain > 0 raises Q, gain < 0 lowers it. The effective resonance is swept at each gain; Γ = π·BW is fitted against the gain. The PLL must be off during the calibration.
